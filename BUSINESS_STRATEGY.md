# NovaOS Business Strategy & Future Policy

## 1. Revenue Model
NovaOS rejects the common "user-as-the-product" model. We do not monetize via ads, data brokerage, or lockscreen "glance" services.
*   **Hardware Margin**: Revenue derived from the sale of Nova-certified hardware.
*   **Optional B2B Services**: Enterprise management tools for organizations requiring high-security mobile fleets.
*   **Nova+ AI (Future)**: Optional premium cloud-based AI features for heavy users, clearly separated from core on-device features.

## 2. Update Policy
Trust is built on longevity.
*   **System Updates**: 3 years of major Android OS updates for all flagship Nova devices.
*   **Security Updates**: 5 years of monthly security patches.
*   **Transparency**: Publicly accessible update trackers and changelogs.

## 3. Data Manifesto
*   **No Forced Accounts**: A Nova Account is never required for core phone functionality (calls, SMS, camera, local AI).
*   **Encrypted by Default**: All user data is encrypted with AES-256-XTS from the first boot.
*   **Local Processing**: We commit to maintaining at least 80% of AI functionality as "Offline-Only".

## 4. Anti-Monetization Commitment
We explicitly reject:
*   Pre-installed "Partner" apps that are not uninstallable.
*   "Suggested" apps in the app drawer or home screen.
*   Search result "Sponsorships" in the system search bar.
*   Diagnostic data sales to third parties.

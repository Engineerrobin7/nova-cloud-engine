# NovaOS Core Principles

NovaOS is built on a foundation of trust, speed, and privacy. These principles are non-negotiable and guide every design and engineering decision we make.

## 1. Zero Advertisements
There are no advertisements in NovaOS. Not in the launcher, not in the settings, not in the lockscreen, and not in any system apps. We believe your phone is a tool, not an ad billboard.

## 2. No Bloatware
All third-party applications included in the build must be fully uninstallable by the user. We do not strike "pay-to-play" deals to pre-install apps that you cannot remove.

## 3. No Dark Patterns
We reject "suggested apps," "hot apps," or auto-installing folders. Your home screen layout is yours and yours alone. We do not use "Glance" style lockscreen layers or notification-shade "recommendations."

## 4. Privacy by Default
NovaOS is designed to be safe out of the box. We minimize data collection and prioritize on-device processing.
- Mic/Camera/Location indicators are prominent.
- Background network access is restricted by default for non-essential apps.
- On-device AI processing is preferred over cloud-dependent services.

## 5. User Sovereignty
You own your hardware. We provide the tools to manage it (NovaDeviceCare, NovaPrivacyCenter) without nagging you or selling your usage patterns.

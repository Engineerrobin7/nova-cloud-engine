# NovaOS Key Features

NovaOS offers a unique blend of privacy, performance, and on-device intelligence.

## 1. Nova Line (AI Search & Command)
Integrated directly into the home screen, the Nova Line allows you to:
- Launch apps with natural language.
- Summarize your recent notifications.
- Control system settings (e.g., "Set a timer for 10 mins", "Turn on Focus Mode").
- Rewrite text in various styles (Formal, Concise).

## 2. Privacy Lab
A suite of tools for advanced privacy control:
- **Privacy Shield**: Automatically blocks known trackers and limits background activity.
- **App Lock**: Biometric protection for sensitive apps.
- **Privacy Vault**: An encrypted space for your most private data.

## 3. On-Device Intelligence
Unlike many AI features that rely on the cloud, NovaOS prioritizes on-device processing to keep your data local.
- **Smart Replies**: Suggested responses in messaging apps.
- **Call Screening**: AI-assisted filtering of spam calls.
- **Aesthetic Adaptation**: System-wide theme and wallpaper suggestions based on your environment.

## 4. Super-Light Optimizer
A performance management tool that:
- Intelligently manages RAM to keep the UI snappy.
- Provides granular battery usage insights.
- Offers performance profiles without "snake-oil" cleaner tactics.
